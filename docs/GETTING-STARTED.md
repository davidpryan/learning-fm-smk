# Learn Firmware: Getting Started

*Learn firmware 0.4, October 2026.*

This tutorial takes you from a fresh install to your first finished lesson in about 15 minutes, on an M-VAVE **SMK-37 Elite** or an **FM-1**. Each device works on its own; you don't need both.

## Before you start

You need the device, a USB data cable, and a computer with desktop Chrome or Edge.

1. Read the safety notes on the install site, then charge the device.
2. **SMK-37 Elite only:** take a full flash backup first (the SMK-37 port kit's backup procedure), and keep the file somewhere safe.
3. Open the install site, [davidpryan.github.io/learning-fm-smk](https://davidpryan.github.io/learning-fm-smk/), and choose the page for **your** device. The installer checks the model and refuses the wrong package.
4. Press **INSTALL** and wait for the device to restart. Keep the cable in and the device on until it does.
5. The device starts in plain SLOOP. Play a few keys to check it sounds.

The two devices print some buttons differently. This tutorial gives both names, **SMK-37 name / FM-1 name**:

| What it does | SMK-37 Elite | FM-1 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Modifier (hold with another control) | GLOBE | GLO |
| Stop everything | PATCH | HOME |
| Start / stop | SEQ PLAY | PLAY |
| Replay / record | SEQ REC | REC |
| Hints on / off | CHORD | ENV |
| Next scale | SCALE | SCL |
| Lessons | ARP | ARP |
| Next page | SEQ | SEQ |
| Volume | K1 (always) | MASTER knob |
| Knobs used by the pages | K2 to K8 | K1 to K7 |

## Step 1: Enter LEARN mode

LEARN mode lives inside SLOOP; you switch to it with one held combination.

1. If SLOOP has a loop you care about, save it to a project slot first. LEARN mode writes into SLOOP's live tracks.
2. Hold **GLOBE + PATCH** / **GLO + HOME** together for **two seconds**. SLOOP's own menu may flash up after a moment; keep holding.
3. The screen shows **WELCOME TO LEARN MODE**. Press any key or button to close it. That press only closes the welcome; it doesn't play a note or do anything else.
4. You are on page 1. The top line shows the key (**C MAJOR**) and **P1/4** on the right: page 1 of 4.
5. Set the volume: **K1** / the **MASTER** knob. On the SMK-37, K1 is always the volume in LEARN mode, whatever the knob layout setting says.

## Step 2: Play and read the screen

Page 1 names everything you play. Try these in order.

1. **Play one key.** The big line shows the note and its octave (for example **C4**). The line under it says what the note is in the key (**ROOT OF THE KEY**, **3RD OF THE KEY**). The key strip lights the key you hold.
2. **Play C, E and G together.** The big line shows the chord (**C**) and the line under it its number in the key (**I IN C MAJOR**).
3. **Play four chords.**
    - SMK-37: press pads **1, 5, 6, 4**. The top row of pads plays the chords of the key; the bottom row plays the same chords with a 7th added.
    - FM-1: press **GLO + SCL** to turn on CHORD KEYS, then press the white keys **C, G, A, F**. Each white key plays its chord. Press GLO + SCL again to turn it off.
4. **Read the chord line.** The orange line now reads **I - V - vi - IV**. Capitals are major chords, lower case are minor, and ° marks a diminished chord. The yellow line at the bottom explains the last chord (**V PULLS BACK TO I**).
5. **Play a black key such as C#.** It isn't in C major, so it is nudged to the nearest note in the key. Press **CHORD** / **ENV** to turn hints off; now off-key notes are silent and the top bar says **C# IS NOT IN C MAJOR**. Press it again to turn hints back on.
6. **Change the key.** Hold **GLOBE** / **GLO** and press a key: that note becomes the root (for example G). Press **SCALE** / **SCL** to step through major, minor, pentatonic and blues. The top line shows the new key, and on the SMK-37 the pad colours change with it.

Use **OCTAVE -/+** / **OCT -/+** to move the keyboard up or down.

## Step 3: Your first lesson

**ARP** starts the next lesson on the page you're on; lesson 1 is **FIRST SOUNDS**. The screen leads you from there.

1. Press **ARP**. The top bar says **LESSON ON (ARP: STOP IT)**, and the purple line shows **L1 FIRST SOUNDS 1/3** (step 1 of 3).
2. An amber bar shows the lesson's text. Read it, then press any key. Do this for both text steps.
3. The echo drill starts. The bar says **ECHO 1/5 LISTEN** while a note plays. Nothing is lit: find it by ear.
4. When the bar turns green (**YOUR TURN**), play the note you heard. Any octave counts. The top bar says **HIT** or **MISS**.
5. After a miss, the screen shows the answer: the key lights, the big line names it and it plays again (**MISS: HERE IT IS**). The next round then starts by itself.
6. After five rounds you get a result card: your score in big digits, and **PASSED** or **NEED 50%**. The **YOU** row shows the notes you played: green if right, red if not.

**If you passed**, the top bar says **L1 DONE! ARP: NEXT, L2**. Press **ARP** for lesson 2.

**If you didn't reach the target**, the step waits for you. The top bar says **NOT YET: NEED 50%** and the prompt says **SEQ PLAY: AGAIN** / **PLAY: AGAIN**. Press it to try that step again. After two misses the screen suggests slow mode: turn **K6** / **K5** to slow the drills down to as little as 50%.

Press **SEQ REC** / **REC** after a round to hear it again: one press plays the reference, the next your attempt.

To choose a different lesson, turn **K7** / **K6** before pressing ARP; the top bar names each lesson, and a **\*** after the name means you've passed it. Press **ARP** during a lesson to stop it. Page 1 has 20 lessons; they go from single notes to chords, keys, sevenths and the blues.

## Step 4: Help, results, the next pages, and leaving

Four combinations get you everywhere else.

- **Help on the screen: GLOBE + SEQ / GLO + SEQ.** Each page has four help screens: what the page is for, its buttons, its knobs and faders, and what its screen shows. **SEQ** goes to the next screen; any other button closes the help. A drill waits while the help is open.
- **Your results: GLOBE + ARP / GLO + ARP.** A numbered list, newest first, of every drill and lesson since power-on, with its score. The same combination closes it.
- **The next page: SEQ.** There are four pages; the top line shows **P1/4** to **P4/4**:
    - **Page 2, Loop it:** drums, chords, bass and a melody in a loop. Press **SEQ PLAY** / **PLAY** to start it.
    - **Page 3, Grow it:** new parts made by a rule from a part you have, and a tape deck for takes.
    - **Page 4, Finish it:** store loops as sections and play them in order as a song.

    Each page has its own lessons: ARP starts the next one.
- **Back to plain SLOOP: hold GLOBE + PATCH / GLO + HOME for two seconds.** The same combination brings you back into LEARN mode.

On the SMK-37, your lesson progress (and the swing and fill unlocks on page 2) is kept when you power off. On the FM-1 it lasts until power-off; use **K6** to pick up where you were. Your results list is cleared at power-off on both.

| Reverb / delay | K4 / K5 | K3 / K4 |

| Next page | SEQ | SEQ |

If the screen ever freezes, hold the LEARN combination for two seconds to get back to plain SLOOP; power off and on if that fails. The full manual ([MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md)) covers every page and control.
